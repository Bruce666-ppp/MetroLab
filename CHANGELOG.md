# Changelog

## 2026-11-28

- Blocked construction when an alignment ends in water. Marked offshore ends in older saves so they can be moved onto land in the line editor.
- Checked the actual click location as well as the final station position before coastal station placement and track snapping.
- Distinguished HKU and Shek Tong Tsui in station suggestions and planned labels using the local MTR station, campus exits and market as geographic references.
- Required new, inserted and relocated stations to be outside water, parks, scenic sites and protected areas in Hong Kong.

## 2.0.0 — 2026-11-27

- Set new games in Hong Kong Island and Kowloon with a real coastline, terrain, buildings, streets and bilingual names. Added zoom-dependent city rendering and geographic layer attribution.
- Added free track planning without stations, at-grade, elevated and underground construction, marine tunnel supplements and HKD construction quotes. Extended track and station editing to approach tracks on lines without service.
- Added Census population, TPEDM job estimates, road-based access, gravity demand calibrated to the local travel survey, fare-sensitive route choice and non-rail alternatives.
- Added line fares, suspension and HKD operating accounts, passenger-flow and employment layers, regional desire lines and network, station and line statistics.
- Added Hong Kong Traditional Chinese, schema-four saves, a simulation worker, deterministic geographic and operations tests and a Hong Kong browser smoke test.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-11-25

- Added the V0.2 cityscape and rail engineering update with switchable 2.5D and plan views, mixed-use buildings, named landmarks and school trips.
- Added direct map-based construction with planned stations, track control points, terminal extensions, automatic interchanges and a live engineering quote.
- Added exact circular rail geometry, the 250 m minimum construction radius, curve speed limits and acceleration and braking profiles shared by trains and passenger routing.
- Added station insertion on one or multiple matching alignments, preserving in-service train positions and passenger groups.
- Added the track engineering layer and geographic naming. Migrated version-one and version-two saves with legacy alignments and active operations intact.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-24

- Added Marlow's river city map, seven land uses, bridges, future development areas and a real operating example network.
- Added Career and Sandbox modes, station construction and upgrades, map-based line drawing, interchange construction, loops and configurable fleets.
- Added time-dependent origin–destination demand, Dijkstra routing, walking access, boarding, transfers, fares and timed city growth.
- Added six map layers, operating charts, financial milestones, bilingual guidance and versioned local saves.
- Added deterministic engine tests, a Chromium gameplay test, bilingual documentation and a GitHub Pages deployment workflow.
