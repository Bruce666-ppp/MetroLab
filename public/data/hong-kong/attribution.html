Hong Kong map sources

The Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and the relevant organisations own the intellectual property rights in their source data. MetroLab uses open data from the CSDI Portal and DATA.GOV.HK. The game crops, reprojects and simplifies the source features.

  • Lands Department buildings, terrain and geographic names. Lands Department spatial data · CSDI terms of use
  • Census and Statistics Department 2021 Census, Planning Department employment estimates and Home Affairs Department boundaries. DATA.GOV.HK terms of use
  • Shoreline, walkable streets, public spaces, supplemental place names and station exclusion polygons from © OpenStreetMap contributors under the Open Database License 1.0. Geofabrik extract
  • HKU and Shek Tong Tsui station naming references. MTR HKU station street map · FEHD facility locations
  • Travel demand calibration based on the Transport Department Travel Characteristics Survey.
